Green Forest Eighteen Strongholds (綠林十八寨) — The Tavern-Brawling Mountain Bandit Chief

A deep dive into Green Forest Eighteen Strongholds, the sect the ESTP type meets in the Jianghu Temperament Test — its origins, signature arts, and best/worst matches.

Origins of the Sect

The Green Forest Eighteen Strongholds is a massive alliance of eighteen bandit camps. To the righteous sects they're nothing but outlaws, but they hold their own code of loyalty and discipline, and they stay closer to the everyday struggles of common people than almost anyone else in the jianghu.

Signature Arts & Weapon

The Heaven-Breaking Single Strike can flip the outcome of a fight with one devastating blow, and the Roaring Tiger Chaos Fist pushes forward with relentless, unrestrained force. Overwhelming power and momentum matter more here than precision.

ESTP Temperament & Green Forest Eighteen Strongholds

The ESTP martial artist matches the bandit chief's wild spirit exactly. Your body reacts before your mind can calculate, and the greater the danger, the more your blood boils — a born fighter. You'd rather learn by diving in headfirst than sit through complicated theory.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Your strength is overwhelming nerve and the drive to break through impossible odds head-on. Your weakness is boredom with long-term planning or careful review, which sometimes leads to rash decisions.

Best Match · Fated Nemesis

You share real warmth beneath the surface with the devoted senior disciple of the Hwasan Sect — a good match — but the careful, contemplative Gonryun sage frustrates you to no end.
