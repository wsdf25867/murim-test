Jongnam Sect (終南派) — The Carefree Wanderer of the Bamboo Grove

A deep dive into Jongnam Sect, the sect the ISFP type meets in the Jianghu Temperament Test — its origins, signature arts, and best/worst matches.

Origins of the Sect

Set among the bamboo groves of Mount Jongnam, the Jongnam Sect is known for its total detachment from worldly fame and profit. It has no interest in the fierce competition between sects, regarding a carefree life spent in nature as the highest achievement of all.

Signature Arts & Weapon

The Carefree Inner Art empties the mind until it becomes one with nature, while the Great Void Flowing Cloud Step moves as light and free as drifting cloud. Lazy-looking most days, but the moment the willow-leaf blade is drawn, nothing is sharper or more precise.

ISFP Temperament & Jongnam Sect

The ISFP martial artist mirrors the Jongnam wanderer's free spirit closely. You value your own quiet, peaceful moments over achievements meant to impress others, and you have little interest in competition or rank. But threaten your space or someone you care about, and you turn in an instant.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Your strength is an ease that isn't rattled by others' opinions, paired with sharp instincts when it matters. Your weakness is losing motivation quickly under excessive pressure or competition, tending to withdraw instead.

Best Match · Fated Nemesis

You strike a good balance with the Bureau's meticulous chief constable, whose thoroughness offsets your ease — but you have almost nothing in common with the conquest-driven leader of the Cheonma Cult.
