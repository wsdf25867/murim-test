Ha-o-mun (下邪魔門) — The Eccentric Sage with a Tongue Sharper Than Any Blade

A deep dive into Ha-o-mun, the sect the ENTP type meets in the Jianghu Temperament Test — its origins, signature arts, and best/worst matches.

Origins of the Sect

Ha-o-mun is a shadow network that controls the information and connections running through every back alley and marketplace. Never recognized as a formal sect, it nonetheless wields an information web broader and more discreet than any established school — impossible to dismiss. It mocks formality and hypocrisy, and its true weapon is a refusal to be bound by any rule.

Signature Arts & Weapon

The Shifting Cloud Step moves the body in unpredictable directions to throw an opponent into confusion, while the Flying Butterfly Iron Fan Strike conjures dozens of improvised variations from a single fan. Ha-o-mun's specialty isn't textbook technique — it's catching an opponent completely off guard.

ENTP Temperament & Ha-o-mun

The ENTP martial artist matches Ha-o-mun's trickster spirit perfectly. You can't stand outdated rules or stiff formality, and you love using sharp wit to argue circles around anyone. Even in a crisis, you find your way out through cleverness and quick improvisation — a born competitor.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Your strength is a knack for unexpected reframes and lightning-fast reflexes. Your weakness is losing interest quickly — you struggle to stick with one thing, especially repetitive training or rigid discipline.

Best Match · Fated Nemesis

You form an intellectually stimulating bond with the Gonryun Sect's reclusive sage, another mind that sees through the surface of the world — but you butt heads constantly with the by-the-book disciples of the Hwasan Sect.
