Martial Alliance (武林盟) — The People's Beacon, Leader of the Righteous Alliance

A deep dive into Martial Alliance, the sect the ENFJ type meets in the Jianghu Temperament Test — its origins, signature arts, and best/worst matches.

Origins of the Sect

The Martial Alliance is a coalition formed by the righteous sects to stand together against threats from the unorthodox and demonic factions. It serves the wellbeing of the entire jianghu rather than any single house's interest, rallying scattered righteous martial artists together whenever the age calls for it.

Signature Arts & Weapon

The Thunderclap Shout rallies allies' morale while breaking an enemy's momentum in a single cry, and the Heaven-Reliant Sovereign Swordplay carries the dignity of someone born to lead others. The Alliance's true power isn't any one person's skill — it's the ability to unite thousands as one.

ENFJ Temperament & Martial Alliance

The ENFJ martial artist matches the Alliance leader's devoted leadership almost exactly. You feel others' pain as your own, and you naturally become the rallying point that draws scattered people together. You don't hesitate to set your own safety aside for the greater cause.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Your strength is an overwhelming charisma that inspires and unites people. Your weakness is being deeply shaken when the very people you trusted and protected betray one another.

Best Match · Fated Nemesis

You form a steady, mutually supportive pair with the Emei Sect's musician, who shares your ideals — but your convictions collide head-on with the secrecy-loving assassin of the Shadow Blade Sect.
